Which company should NexaShare follow?

Provide the LinkedIn company page for the company you work for. This will be the first company NexaShare watches for new content to repost.

Open the company on LinkedIn and copy the full page address.

Automatic reposting will be enabled for this company. NexaShare records a repost as successful only after LinkedIn visibly confirms it. You can pause the company later in Settings.
