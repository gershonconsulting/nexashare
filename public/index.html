How it works

From your sources to verified shares.

1

Connect your workspace

Sign in with LinkedIn, add your sources, and choose what NexaShare should follow.

2

Set up your extension

Install the private Chrome extension and keep Chrome running and signed in to LinkedIn.

3

Review your outcomes

Run a controlled test, then use activity and reports to review confirmed reposts and any failures.

Your NexaShare Chrome extension

Private testing build. Download the ZIP, extract it, and load the folder with Chrome’s Load unpacked option. The dashboard guides you through connection and updates.
